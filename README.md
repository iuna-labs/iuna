# iuna

iuna is an experimental L1 cryptocurrency designed to keep network power temporary. It separates block selection, timing, and coin issuance through a proof-of-burn lottery, verifiable delay functions, and proof-of-work mining.

iuna currently runs as a **candidate mainnet**. It is not mainnet yet, and the protocol remains experimental. The current chain is intended to carry forward if the candidate is promoted, so coins will probably remain valid—but that is not guaranteed.

## Why iuna?

Many networks give lasting advantages to whoever already controls the most capital or mining scale:

- **Proof of Work:** specialized ASIC hardware and economies of scale give large miners an advantage over smaller participants.
- **Proof of Stake:** more capital brings more influence, which can generate more rewards and reinforce that influence.
- **iuna's Proof of Burn:** burning $10 gives a small holder the same temporary lottery weight as it gives a large holder. The coins are spent, the influence expires, and it must be paid for again.

iuna combines this temporary block-selection power with openly mineable coin issuance and verifiable delay functions for timing.

## Contributing

iuna is an open experiment, and contributions of all kinds are welcome:

- Open a [pull request](https://github.com/iuna-labs/iuna/pulls) or [issue](https://github.com/iuna-labs/iuna/issues).
