Getting started with iuna

Choose how your node participates based on your device and how much power you want it to use. You can change mining and burning later.

Download iuna Choose a setup
01 · The basics

Mining and burning do different jobs

They can run together, but neither one is required just to keep a node online and synchronized.

Mine

Create new IUNA

Your CPU searches for proof-of-work mine actions. Faster hardware finds more opportunities; older hardware can still participate, only more slowly.

Burn

Finalize blocks and earn fees

You permanently spend existing IUNA for temporary tickets. A winning ticket lets you finalize a block and receive transaction fees; tickets can also let an eligible mined lineage serve on the burn committee.

02 · Choose a setup

Match participation to your device

These are practical starting points, not profitability promises. Watch the live guidance in the node and adjust as the network changes.

◫
Small or older hardware

Mine slowly, burn low

  • Mining is fine if the extra power use is acceptable, but expect it to be slow.
  • Use the lowest practical burn amount instead of competing aggressively for blocks.
  • This is better suited to committee participation: committee weight comes from mature mined-coin lineage, not from a larger burn.
◆
Modern hardware

Mine and follow max-EV

  • Enable mining to search continuously for new IUNA.
  • For burning, use the node's max-EV guidance when competing burn exists.
  • Max-EV is a risk-neutral estimate from current conditions—not a guarantee of winning a block or earning a return.
◒
Low-energy device

Burn only

  • Turn mining off to avoid continuous proof-of-work use.
  • Enable burning only when you want to participate with IUNA you already own.
  • A laptop on battery can stay synchronized without mining or opening an inbound port.
03 · Help the network

An open port is optional—and valuable

No inbound port required

A non-listening node connects out to peers, synchronizes normally, and keeps inbound P2P closed. This is the easiest option behind restrictive routers or on a mobile connection.

Listening nodes make iuna stronger

If you can accept inbound connections, select Listening node during setup and make its P2P port reachable. The default is TCP 9444. Expose only P2P—keep the management interface private.

Ready?

Run a node and start with the conservative option.

You can change mining, burn amount, and public-node settings from the interface later.
