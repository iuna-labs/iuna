Why this matters
Why iuna

Influence you have to keep earning

In most networks, whoever already controls the most hardware or capital keeps the most say. iuna tests a different balance: Heartbeat mining stays open to anyone, and Ledger mining weight has to be paid for again and again. A ticket's budget is not staked but spent: it goes to Heartbeat miners, is never refunded, and the weight it buys expires within a day.

No scale advantage

Every coin spent buys the same weight, whether you hold a little or a lot. Being bigger never makes a coin count for more: weight grows only in step with what you pay.

Open entry

Anyone can mine SHA-256 Heartbeats. Ticket budgets and Ledger fees reward the work they publish.

An experiment, not a promise

The design is public so its assumptions can be tested, challenged and improved with evidence.

How it works

Two chains, one shared state

A Proof-of-Work Heartbeat Chain keeps the clock. A Ledger Chain settles payments through a lottery whose tickets are bought with Proof of Expenditure. Every full node validates both, plus their shared UTXO state.

Help test

The network needs independent nodes

iuna can only be judged by running it. Every node on a different machine, network or operating system makes the network more honest.

  1. Install and start a node

    Download the desktop app for macOS or Windows, or the node for Linux. You can also build it from source with cargo run --release.

    Download iuna

  2. Let it sync and stay online

    The node finds peers and validates both chains from the very first block. If you can, allow inbound TCP port 9444 so other nodes can reach yours.

  3. Report what you see

    Crashes, stuck syncs, odd balances, confusing screens: post them on Discord or open an issue on GitHub.

    Join Discord Open an issue

FAQ

Before you start

Is this mainnet?

Not yet. iuna runs as a candidate mainnet and the protocol remains experimental. The chain is intended to carry forward if the candidate is promoted, but that is not guaranteed. Treat any IUNA you hold as part of a test.

Can I buy IUNA?

This site does not sell IUNA. New coins come from taking part in the Ledger lottery: the winning lottery ticket settles the next Ledger block and receives its block reward.

The target supply is 42,076,801 IUNA, with the Ledger reward halving about every four years.

How is this different from Proof of Work?

In Proof of Work, whoever runs the most hashrate decides which blocks get made, and specialized hardware and cheap power make the biggest miners ever more efficient.

iuna still uses Proof of Work, but only for Heartbeats: they keep time, order activity and supply randomness. Who settles payments in the Ledger is decided by a lottery among mining tickets, not by hashrate. Ticket budgets are paid to Heartbeat miners, so ticket buyers pay for the work that secures the clock.

How is this different from Proof of Stake?

Staked coins stay yours and keep earning influence. A mining ticket's budget is spent: it is paid to Heartbeat miners, never refunded, and the weight it bought is gone after at most about a day. To keep influence, you have to keep paying for it.

Do I need special hardware?

No, and you do not need computing power to take part in mining. With IUNA you can buy a mining ticket instead: if it wins the lottery, your node signs the next Ledger block, settling its payments, and receives the block reward and fees. Keep your node online so it can sign when your ticket wins.

If you do want to search for Heartbeats, an ordinary computer can: they use SHA-256 and difficulty adjusts to the hashrate on the network. And even without mining, an online, validating node already helps.

Is the code open?

Yes. The node, desktop app, wallet and this website are open source under the Apache 2.0 license, on GitHub.
