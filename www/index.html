The idea

What are we trying to change?

Many networks give lasting influence to whoever already controls the most capital or mining scale. iuna tests a different balance: open coin issuance, lightweight finalization, and influence that expires.

Temporary influence

No permanent seat at the table

Finalization power is paid for again each time. Past participation does not become a permanent claim on the network.

Open entry

New supply stays mineable

Anyone can search for proof-of-work mine actions. Issuance is kept separate from block finalization.

Still unproven

An experiment, not a promise

The design is public so its assumptions can be tested, challenged, and improved with evidence.

Under the hood

Three mechanisms, three different jobs

iuna separates coin issuance, block selection, and timing instead of asking one scarce resource to control everything.

01 · Issue

Proof of work creates IUNA

Open proof-of-work mine actions introduce new coins without deciding who finalizes the next block.

02 · Select

Burns enter a block lottery

Nodes permanently spend IUNA for a temporary chance to finalize. That influence must be paid for again.

03 · Time

A verifiable delay sets the pace

Sequential work creates a checkable clock and gives fallback finalizers a predictable way to take over.

Building in public

Progress you can verify

The candidate is not presented as finished. Its rules, release gates, security work, and remaining launch blockers are documented in public.

Current phase

Live mainnet candidate

The candidate ledger is running now and may later be promoted without replacing its history. That outcome is deliberately not guaranteed.

Trust through inspection

Do not take the claims on faith

iuna is designed to be inspected. The code, rules, known risks, and launch criteria are available before you decide to participate.

No guarantees

Treat value as uncertain

The candidate can fail, require intervention, or never gain economic value. Participation is for testing the experiment.
